A study platform with smooth UI, easy navigation, and features designed to help students prepare for exams effectively with AI chat integration and simulator experiences.

This platform needed to handle complex functionality: CBT simulators, AI chat for limited responses, light/dark theme toggles, and multiple exam sections. While remaining intuitive for students under pressure.

I focused on navigation clarity, reducing cognitive load, and ensuring the interface felt encouraging rather than overwhelming. The result was a platform students actually wanted to return to.
